PRIVACY

BillFlow Privacy Notice

Release-candidate notice. The operator must review and complete this notice before public commercial release.

Information stored on your device

BillFlow stores business profile details, customer details, invoices, quotes, receipts and payment records in browser storage on the device and browser profile you use. This prototype does not provide a BillFlow account or cloud synchronization service. The app's local storage is not encrypted by BillFlow.

Sharing and printing

If you choose to share a document through WhatsApp, email or another service, or print it or save it as a PDF, information you choose to share may be handled by that service or saved to your device. Review documents and recipients before sharing. The privacy practices of third-party services apply to their handling of information.

Backups and data retention

Exported JSON backups may contain personal and business information, including customer and payment records. Store backup files securely and share them only with trusted recipients. Anyone who gains access to an exported backup may be able to read its contents.

Browser data may be lost if site data is cleared, a browser profile is removed, or the device is lost or damaged. Export backups regularly and verify that important backups can be restored.

Data transmission and third-party services

BillFlow is designed as a local-first prototype and does not include a BillFlow-hosted account or cloud database. Sharing features may open external applications or services. Actual network behavior should be verified before release, and this notice must be updated if hosting, analytics, external APIs, or other data-processing services are introduced.

Current limitations

This prototype does not provide user accounts, cloud sync, encrypted account storage, a multi-user access-control system, or automatic sending of invoices and reminders. Access to records depends on the browser storage and backup files available to you.

Contact and operator information

Before public release, the operator must add the verified business or operator name, a monitored privacy contact address, and any other disclosures required for the intended markets.
